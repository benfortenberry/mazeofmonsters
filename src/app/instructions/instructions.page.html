How to Escape

15 minutes to escape Yabdib's maze, or you join the collection.

The rules

  • Move with the arrows. They appear only where the way is open.
  • Turn around to head back the way you came.
  • Open the map to see where you've been. Beating a monster reveals the way ahead.
  • Monsters block your way. Beat their game for help. Lose, and it costs you time.
  • Watch the timer. If it runs out, the maze claims you.

Keyboard shortcuts

↑/W Move forward
←/A Turn left
→/D Turn right
↓/S Turn around
M Open the map
C Check your heading
T Check the timer
H/? Open this help
Enter Dismiss a prompt

Reach the end before the clock does. Good luck.
