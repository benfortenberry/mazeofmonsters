How to Escape

You have 15 minutes to find your way out of the maze before you're turned into one of the monster's creations.

  • Use the arrows to move. They only appear where the way is open — walls block your path.
  • Turn around with the refresh button to head back the way you came.
  • Open the map to see where you've been. Winning a monster's challenge adds rooms to it.
  • Monsters block your way. Beat their mini-game for help — lose, and you join the collection.
  • Keep an eye on the timer. If it runs out, the maze claims you.

Reach the end before the clock does. Good luck.
